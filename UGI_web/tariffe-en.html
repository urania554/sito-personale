Services and fees by category

Parking, canteen and uniforms work slightly differently for employees, trainees/visiting staff and freelancers. Below is a summary; further down you can look up your role in the full service matrix.

Parking: fee with your badge

For employees (and anyone marked “see category” in the matrix) the fee depends on the contract category and is valid for 24 hours. Freelancers, outpatient specialists and medical residents pay € 2.00.

Parking: the Interparking agreements

If you can't park with your badge (trainees, PhD students, students, volunteers…) there is an agreement with the car park operator: you need an authorisation letter and use a rechargeable card.

How the card works (both agreements)

  • Where to collect it: cash office of the Multipiano-Sud car park (P4), green level, sector 1, with an ID document. The operator checks you are on the list and gives you the card with zero credit.
  • Car parks: North (P1) and East (P3).
  • How to use it: on entry and exit, without taking a ticket.
  • Top-up: at the car park pay machines, or at the main cash desk by credit or debit card. Your remaining credit is shown every time you use it.
  • Note: keep the card away from magnetic fields. A replacement costs € 5.00 if you lose it. Unused credit is not refunded.
  • Information: Interparking, tel. 035 5785057 · info-bhp@interparking.com
